About Me

Dexian | IIT Madras

I'm a Senior Software Engineer at Dexian, focused on developing proof-of-concept solutions that validate and showcase the effectiveness of innovative proposals. My expertise spans machine learning, deep learning, NLP, and LLM-based applications, with a proven track record of deploying scalable solutions on Azure cloud platforms.

As an MS research scholar at IIT Madras, I specialized in Audio and Speech Processing within the Speech, Music and Vision Lab under Prof. Hema A. Murthy. My research concentrated on incorporating expression and prosody into conversational speech synthesis using hybrid statistical-parametric and neural-based TTS models.

I've contributed significantly to government-sponsored initiatives including the Bhashini multi-lingual project by the Government of India and the NLTM (National Language Translation Mission) pilot, focusing on speech synthesis and transcreation of lectures into Indian languages with isochronous lip-syncing.
